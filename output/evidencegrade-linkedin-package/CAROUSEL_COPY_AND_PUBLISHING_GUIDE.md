## Claim discipline
### Source-grounded
- EvidenceGrade is described by OpenEvidence as a real-time, GRADE-inspired assessment for applicable clinical AI answers.
- It uses per-paper quality, certainty and relevance assessments.
- Its reported outputs include A–D, plus/minus modifiers and U (Unable to Grade).
- Body-level evaluation considers guidelines/systematic reviews, study-design ceilings, and upgrades/downgrades.

### Editorial analysis / potential limitations
- Retrieval coverage can affect the final grade.
- Automated appraisal and relevance assessments are context-sensitive.
- A single letter can hide the reason evidence is uncertain.
- Independent validation and calibration are important.

These are intentionally presented as considerations, not verified defects of the product.
