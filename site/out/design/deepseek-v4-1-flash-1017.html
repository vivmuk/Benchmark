01 — Onboard

Built for the long, dark hours.

Three carriages, forty-eight berths, and a lounge that faces the dunes. There are no screens on the walls — the windows do all the work.

01

Sleeping cabins

Private berths with pressed linen, a brass reading lamp, and a window that opens onto the dunes. Blackout curtains for when the moon is too much.

02

Dune-view lounge

The last carriage is all glass and low light. Tea at midnight, a bar cart at one, and nothing but sand and stars until the horizon changes colour.

03

Neon arrival

At 07:20 the skyline appears: magenta, cyan, and a hundred towers of it. Breakfast is served in the lounge exactly as the city comes into view.

02 — Timetable

Four departures, every night.

All services run after sundown and arrive with the sunrise. Berthing opens forty minutes before departure — the lounge stays open until the last of the sand disappears.
