01 / Life on board

The hours in between.

No connections. No rushing the view.
Just a night on the rails, well spent.

A / 01

A window to the dark

Wide observation windows frame the last light on the dunes. Later, the lounge lights dim for a sky full of stars.

B / 02

The midnight table

Take a booth in the dining car for spiced flatbread, a late supper, or a pot of mint tea as the desert rolls by.

C / 03

Sleep past the miles

Settle into a quiet carriage with a reading lamp of your own. Wake to soft chimes and the first towers of Luma.
