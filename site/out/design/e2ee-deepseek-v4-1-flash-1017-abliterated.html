On board

Three reasons to leave the road behind.

Dune Line is one train, eleven carriages, and a route that has not changed since 1974. Here is what the night buys you.

01

A door, a bed, a window

Every fare includes a real mattress behind a real door. Linen changed at Ashfall, a brass reading lamp, and glass cut wide enough for the dunes to go blue at two in the morning.

02

Sky-deck lounge

The last carriage has almost no roof to speak of. Sit under the stars with a cardamom coffee and the wind off the sand until the city glow rises on the horizon.

03

Arrive rested, not wrecked

Breakfast is served ninety minutes out. Showers, pressed shirts and a porter who learns your name before Mirage Junction — so you walk into Vega Gate looking like you flew.
