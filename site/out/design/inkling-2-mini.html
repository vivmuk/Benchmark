Why Dune Line

Built for the dark hours,
between light and light.

Three quiet revolutions that make the overnight crossing unlike any other ride in the world.

Sealed Sleeper Cabins

Blackout glazing, twin climate zones and a 180° bed pitched for desert chill. Wake hours from home with the city light in your eyes — never once woke by traffic.

The Glass-Dune Crossing

The line rides its own thermal ridge above the dunes at 168 km/h — smooth, silent, and 70 minutes faster than the bypass road. No traffic, no headlights, no excess.

Neon City Arrival

Step off into light: the arcology's garden terraces, a zero-wait transfer to the grid, and a 12-minute walk from platform to the first neon sign.
