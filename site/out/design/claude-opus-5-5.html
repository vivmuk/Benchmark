The journey

Built for the slow way across.

Eleven hours, one long horizon. We run quiet electric trains on a single desert line, timed so the dunes are golden as you board and the city is glowing as you wake.

01

Skylight sleepers

Every berth has a roof window tilted to the southern sky. Cabin lights dim at 23:00, and the Milky Way does the rest.

Blackout blinds · Desert-linen bedding
02

The Oasis dining car

Supper of dates, saffron and slow smoke, cooked on board and served as the train crests the great dunes at Qasr Ridge.

Served 20:30 – 23:00 · Bar till late
03

Arrive at first light

Roll into Lumora as the neon fades into sunrise. Hot shower, strong coffee, and your luggage waiting on the platform.

Arrival 06:40 · Lumora Central
