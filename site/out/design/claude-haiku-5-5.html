The journey

Three things the desert gives back

Eleven hours with nothing to do but watch the dunes turn from gold to silver. We built the train around that quiet.

01

Sleeper berths that feel like rooms

Every berth has blackout linen, a reading lamp and a window that opens onto the dunes. The bed folds down at dusk and stays made until the desert ends.

2.0 mberth length
Quietcar, 24 hrs
02

An observation car with no ceiling lights

The rear car is glass from floor to dome. Lights are dimmed after midnight, so the Milky Way comes through and the dunes glow faintly underneath.

360°glass dome
Openall night
03

Dusk dining, cooked for the cold hours

Slow braises, flatbreads from the onboard oven and mint tea poured at the table. The galley closes at 3 a.m., and a warm supper is always waiting for late boarders.
