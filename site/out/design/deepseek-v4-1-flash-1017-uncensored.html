The crossing

Six hundred kilometres of quiet.

Four stops, one long night, and a great deal of nothing in between — which is precisely the point.

Berths, not benches

Every cabin is a private berth with linen, a brass reading lamp and a door that actually closes. Ten hours of sleep you'll be glad you had.

The Saffron Car

Dinner at ten under low brass lamps: flatbread, apricot stew, cardamom coffee. One sitting, long table, strangers who stop being strangers.

Arrive at first glow

The last dune clears at dawn as the city switches itself on. Wake-up tea at 05:50, no rush, no scramble for a taxi rank.
