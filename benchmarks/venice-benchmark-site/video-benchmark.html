Video Models

Every text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video model on the Venice catalog, each generating at its minimum duration and resolution — categorized by model type, so each is tested for what it actually does.

Fixed prompts — identical for every model in the category

Realism: A golden retriever running through a sunlit field of tall grass at golden hour, slow motion, cinematic depth of field, photorealistic, warm light

Creative: A tiny astronaut sailing a paper boat across a puddle that reflects a galaxy, whimsical miniature world, macro shot, dreamy lighting

Image-to-Video: the subject slowly turns her head toward the camera and smiles, hair sways gently in the breeze, subtle camera push-in, cinematic lighting

Reference-to-Video: the person comes alive, turns toward the camera, subtle blink and expression change, photorealistic
