Grok 4.6 verdict — winner: claude-fable-5-1

Opus 4.8 Fast S 81 · D 86

Elegant magazine essay with a clean LLM-vs-world-model distinction, six applications, a structural-shift table, and a strong 'hypothesis engine not truth engine' close. Prose is polished but thinner than the others: applications stay high-level, no ML lineage, few concrete rollouts. Design is restrained and typographically excellent (drop cap, gold quotes, definition box, 01–05 numbering) but less distinctive than the two Fable pieces.

Fable 5 S 87 · D 88

Sharper strategic argument than Opus: evidence as portfolio optimization, counterfactual impact as the missing CFO metric, 'astrology with a GPU budget.' Six applications plus four cards, four identity shifts, three failure modes. Memorable lines throughout. Still less function-by-function and less technically grounded than 5.1. Design is cohesive teal/gold/cream with a strong dark hero, card grid, and well-crafted table—more visual personality than Opus, slightly less ambitious than 5.1.

Fable 5.1 S 95 · D 93

Best answer to applications + transformation by a wide margin: encoder/dynamics/decoder definition, Ha–Schmidhuber/Dreamer/JEPA lineage, hybrid-systems honesty for 2026, ten function-level applications with specific rollouts (cardiometabolic evidence policy, belief-dynamics ethics, MSL transcript), then epistemology/roles/metrics/org politics. Failure modes and a 2026–2029 sequence are unusually serious. Design is the most crafted—night masthead, Observe→Act loop, CSS-counter apps, mono rehearsal, timeline, print CSS—serving the argument rather than decorating it.

claude-fable-5-1 wins both axes: it is the only piece that treats world models as a real ML object, maps them onto medical affairs function by function with testable examples, and then follows through on transformation (plan-as-prediction, insights-as-state, custodianship of a shared disease model) plus governance and a staged build path. claude-fable-5 is the runner-up—punchier and more economically sharp than Opus, with a more distinctive UI, but still an essay of theses rather than a working map. Opus 4.8 Fast is the most classically written of the three and a very competent editorial layout, yet it stays one abstraction layer above the job.
